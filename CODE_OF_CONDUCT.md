# Code of Conduct

## The short version

Be decent. Assume the other person is acting in good faith until they show you
otherwise.

## What that means in practice

Criticise code, not people. "This function has a race condition" is useful.
"Whoever wrote this doesn't understand threading" is not, and it is usually
wrong anyway. Most bad code was written by someone reasonable under
constraints you cannot see.

Harassment, personal attacks, and demeaning comments about anyone's identity
are not welcome here and will get you removed.

If you are new to this and ask something obvious, that is fine. Everyone was
new. If someone else asks something obvious, answer it or say nothing.

## Reporting

Open an issue, or write to the address on the maintainer's GitHub profile if
it is something you would rather not post publicly. Reports are read and taken
seriously.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, remove, or reject comments, commits, issues, and pull
requests that do not fit the above, and will explain why when they do.
Repeated or serious problems mean a ban.
